Terms & Learning Rules

Please review and accept our rules before updating your student profile.

1. The Spaced Repetition Protocol

To ensure vocabulary retention, cards must be reviewed exactly when they are due. If you miss a review session, overdue cards will reset back to Box 1.


2. Academic Integrity

User account sharing is strictly prohibited. Local databases and course contents are watermarked and encrypted to protect copyrighted publications.


3. Backup and Sync Policies

User-created custom cards are stored locally on this device. Purchased catalog courses are backed up and synced off-server immediately upon network check-in.
